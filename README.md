# DWCC Sports Management System

## 📌 About
The **DWCC Sports Management System** is a web-based application designed to help manage sports teams, student-athletes, coaches, training schedules, attendance, performance evaluations, and competitions at Divine Word College of Calapan.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Athlete Management** – Register, update, and manage athlete information.
- **Team Management** – Organize teams, sports, and coach assignments.
- **Attendance Tracking** – Record athlete attendance during training sessions.
- **Performance Evaluation** – Rate athlete performance and generate remarks.
- **Training Schedules** – Manage training dates, locations, and activities.
- **Competition Management** – Organize competitions and track team participation and results.
- **User Authentication** – Provide role-based access for administrators, managers, and coaches.
- **Dashboard and Reports** – Support sports management through organized records and summaries.
